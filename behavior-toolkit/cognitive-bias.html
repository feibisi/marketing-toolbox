认知偏差清单

Cognitive Bias Checklist
稀缺与紧迫 (Scarcity)
Scarcity Effect (稀缺效应)
We value things more when they are rare or limited.
Loss Aversion (损失厌恶)
Pain of losing is twice as powerful as pleasure of gaining.
社会影响 (Social)
Social Proof (社会认同)
We copy the actions of others in uncertain situations.
Authority Bias (权威偏误)
We trust and follow the advice of experts.
决策辅助 (Decision)
Anchoring (锚定效应)
The first piece of information influences all subsequent judgments.
Decoy Effect (诱饵效应)
Introducing a 3rd option to make one of the others more attractive.
认知流畅 (Ease)
Default Effect (默认效应)
We tend to stick with pre-set options.
Hick's Law (希克定律)
More choices = more time/effort to decide.
互惠承诺 (Commitment)
Reciprocity (互惠原则)
We feel obliged to return favors.
Foot-in-the-door (登门槛)
Agreeing to a small request increases likelihood of larger one.
情感视觉 (Emotion)
Von Restorff (隔离效应)
Items that stand out are more likely to be remembered.
Halo Effect (光环效应)
Positive impression in one area influences opinion in others.
