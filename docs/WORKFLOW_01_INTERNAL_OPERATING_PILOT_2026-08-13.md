## Purpose and boundary

This pilot uses the Marketing Toolbox project itself to run all six Strategy Alignment methods in sequence. It tests whether the workflow can hold a coherent internal decision using repository and governance facts.

It is **not** a customer case, market validation, willingness-to-pay test, domain-method approval, `fixture` readiness, or release approval. It contains no customer, order, revenue, advertising-export or personal data. The machine-readable record is `data/pilots/workflow01-marketing-toolbox-internal.json`.

- Product inventory and workflow assignments: `data/tools.json`, `data/workflows.json`.
- Current support depth: `data/core-readiness.json`.

- Evidence/handoff implementation: `shared/js/workflow-panel.js`, `shared/js/workflow-store.js`.
- Product direction: `docs/PRODUCT_VISION_AND_DEVELOPMENT_DIRECTION.md`.

Statements without a qualifying source are marked as assumptions and carry an explicit source-gap explanation in the pilot JSON.

## Six-step result

1. **SWOT:** the project has broad inventory, working quality gates and a defined WordPress acquisition boundary, but support depth and demand evidence lag page count.
2. **Landscape:** the working differentiation hypothesis is local provenance plus ordered handoffs, not “more templates.” Competitor and substitute positions remain internal hypotheses, not market facts.
3. **Business model:** the candidate offer is self-service local tooling plus an optional reviewed project service. Pricing, demand and delivery ownership are not approved.
4. **Value proposition:** the candidate promise is a sourced, reviewable plan without compulsory account/cloud transfer. No external run proves completion or preference.
5. **OKR:** progress is measured by behavior-level method coverage and at least one authorized, sanitised, domain-reviewed run—not page count.
6. **Roadmap:** method behavior and sample intake proceed independently, then converge in a six-tool reviewed run. Workflow 02 remains gated behind that evidence.

## Decision

Continue Workflow 01 hardening. Do not add more supported tools, promote any core tool to `fixture`, advertise customer validation, or begin Workflow 02 business implementation yet.

The next admissible business evidence is one to three project samples with recorded authorization, sanitisation responsibility, allowed purpose, retention boundary and named domain reviewer. An internal sample can test operations but cannot replace customer evidence.
