## SWOT analysis

**Source basis**

- University of Kansas Community Tool Box: SWOT identifies internal strengths/weaknesses and broader opportunities/threats for planning and decision-making.
  https://ctb.ku.edu/en/table-of-contents/assessment/assessing-community-needs-and-resources/swot-analysis/main

**Source-backed rules**

- Separate internal factors from external factors.
- Use the method to support strategic planning and decision-making.
- Turn the four quadrants into a structure that can be revisited and refined.

**Project inference**

- Every item carries evidence kind, source, owner, and review date.
- The conclusion must name one prioritized strategic implication instead of repeating the list.
- The handoff target is the next workflow step, not a standalone SWOT summary.

**Rights / naming note**

- SWOT is a common framework name; no special reuse license was verified on the cited page during this review.

## Competitive landscape map

**Source basis**

- Virginia Tech strategic management text: strategic group maps use two axes, chosen from competitive factors, to plot rival groups and reveal closest rivals and gaps.
  https://pressbooks.lib.vt.edu/strategicmanagement/chapter/3-5-mapping-strategic-groups/
- Washington State University open textbook: a perceptual map is a two-dimensional graph showing where a product stands relative to competitors using buyer-important criteria.
  https://wsu.pressbooks.pub/mktg360/chapter/6-9/

**Source-backed rules**

- Use two dimensions that matter to competition or buyer perception.
- Plot competitors relative to each other, not as a free-form brainstorm list.
- Use the map to spot clusters, closest rivals, and untapped gaps.

**Project inference**

- Axis labels must include unit and direction.
- The page must distinguish competitor, substitute, and own offer.
- At least three comparable alternatives are required; otherwise the page must state why the map is not applicable.

**Rights / naming note**

- The Virginia Tech textbook page is published under a Creative Commons BY-NC-SA license; the WSU page is a quoted course text. Keep source attribution and do not imply that the combined tool is an official standard.

## Business Model Canvas

**Source basis**

- Strategyzer: a business model is a representation of how an organization makes or intends to make money, described through nine building blocks.
  https://www.strategyzer.com/library/what-is-a-business-model
- Strategyzer: the canvas is a strategic management and entrepreneurial tool for describing, designing, challenging, inventing, and pivoting a business model.
  https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-business-model-canvas

**Source-backed rules**

- Model the business as a simplified representation, not a complete operating manual.
- Keep the nine building blocks together so the trade-offs are visible.
- Treat the canvas as a tool for design and challenge, not just documentation.

**Project inference**

- Each block records assumptions and dependencies.
- Revenue and cost statements must name the unit or economic basis.
- Contradictions across blocks must be surfaced explicitly instead of smoothed over.

**Rights / naming note**

- Business Model Canvas is a Strategyzer-branded method name; cite Strategyzer and do not claim ownership of the framework.

## Value Proposition Canvas

**Source basis**

- Strategyzer: the canvas helps define customer jobs, pains, and gains, then describe products and services, pain relievers, and gain creators.
  https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-value-proposition-canvas
- Strategyzer: the value proposition canvas is a plug-in to the business model canvas and is used to build product-market fit.
  https://www.strategyzer.com/value-proposition

**Source-backed rules**

- Start from customer jobs, pains, and gains.
- Map the offering to how it relieves pain and creates gains.
- Use the canvas to test and refine fit.

**Project inference**

- Customer jobs, pains, and gains must be ranked.
- Each product response must map to a specific customer item.
- The page must preserve at least one unresolved mismatch so risk is visible to the next step.

**Rights / naming note**

- Value Proposition Canvas is a Strategyzer-branded method name; cite Strategyzer and do not imply a reusable license was verified beyond the source pages.

## OKR cascade

**Source basis**

- Google re:Work: OKRs are Google’s cornerstone goal-setting method, intended to set ambitious goals and track progress; objectives are ambitious, key results are measurable, and KRs should describe outcomes rather than activities.
  https://rework.withgoogle.com/intl/en/guides/set-goals-with-okrs
- Atlassian: OKRs align teams around measurable objectives and outcomes.
  https://www.atlassian.com/agile/agile-at-scale/okr

**Source-backed rules**

- Keep objectives ambitious and outcome-oriented.
- Keep key results measurable and graded with a number.
- Use OKRs to create alignment, not as a task list.

**Project inference**

- Every objective needs baseline, target, unit, due date, owner, and data source.
- Activity-only key results are invalid.
- Each objective must trace back to the chosen strategy, not a generic company wish list.

**Rights / naming note**

- OKR is a common management framework name; the source pages describe the method, but no special trademark or reuse restriction was verified here.

## Strategic roadmap

**Source basis**

- Atlassian: a product roadmap outlines vision, direction, priorities, and progress over time and should be updated as priorities change.
  https://www.atlassian.com/agile/product-management/product-roadmaps

**Source-backed rules**

- Present the plan of action over time.
- Keep teams aligned on short- and long-term goals.
- Update the roadmap when priorities shift.

**Project inference**

- Each initiative needs an outcome link, owner, dependency, and start/end window.
- Every committed initiative must map back to an OKR key result.
- Dependency cycles are invalid and must be named.

**Rights / naming note**

- Roadmap is a common planning term; the Atlassian page is used as the method reference for this workflow.
